Featured Capstone Project

Project DALOY

Drainage Assessment and Level Observation Yielding AI-Early-Warning System

IoT & AI Integration

Smart Urban Flood Monitoring

Project DALOY combines an ESP32 microcontroller, HC-SR04 ultrasonic water-level sensor, YF-S201 flow sensor, and ESP32-CAM to gather physical and visual information from open drainage areas. Camera data is processed via AI to detect visible debris, while sensor readings trigger normal, warning, or critical alerts over Wi-Fi.

Real-time Wi-Fi Dashboard
AI Debris Detection

Process Pipeline

1 Sense & Collect: Ultrasonic & Flow sensors track water levels.
2 Process & Detect: ESP32-CAM captures imagery for AI debris analysis.
3 Respond & Monitor: Alerts sent to dashboard for proactive maintenance.
