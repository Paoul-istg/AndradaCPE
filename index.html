About Me

I am Paoul Jarred Andrada, a 1st-Year BS Computer Engineering student at Mapúa University. My passion lies in understanding how hardware microcontrollers and software algorithms communicate seamlessly to solve engineering problems.

Throughout my first year, I have focused on building a solid technical foundation in fundamental programming languages including C/C++, Java, and HTML, alongside visual algorithmic logic through Scratch coding.

I complement software logic with hands-on microcontroller hardware prototyping using Arduino systems and Raspberry Pi single-board computers, allowing me to build functional physical systems like automated hazard sensors and AI early-warning modules.

First-Year Experience

Facing the Reality

Entering Mapúa University was a powerful wake-up call. It was a challenging transition where I was hit by the reality of engineering—fast-paced terms, demanding problem sets, and dense technical requirements.

However, despite the intense workload and steep learning curve, I was truly enjoying my time. Doing these courseworks and creating Project DALOY with my peers turned initial shock into genuine enthusiasm for engineering.

Personal Reflection

Accepting the Challenge

"Just because you are smart in math and science doesn't mean that engineering will be easy for you."

Mapúa hit me with that reality, and I am humble enough to accept the challenge. High school math and science competence is only a foundation—true engineering requires perseverance, resilience, and constant iteration. Accepting this reality has shaped my drive to grow as a student engineer.
